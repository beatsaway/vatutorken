（三）個人文字簡介（可英語授課 · 中英對照）

中文

黃浩均（Kenneth Ho Kwan Wong）畢業於香港理工大學視覺傳達設計，其後於英國愛丁堡大學取得聲音設計碩士，並在英國伯明翰大學完成中學設計與科技科 PGCE，現為香港註冊教師及英國合資格教師（QTS）。

藝術方面，他早年修讀視覺藝術，於 HKCEE 與 HKAL 視覺藝術科皆考獲 A 級，先以藝術創作為起點，其後再延伸及平面與多媒體設計，並長期從事跨領域創作；亦曾於英國中學任設計與科技教師及班主任，熟悉課堂管理、專業繪圖、評估與照顧多元學習需要。2026 年回港後於本港中學任教視覺藝術，指導非華語學生完成 HKDSE SBA 及課業紀錄，教授超現實主義、立體主義與極簡主義等課題。

擬任教科目：多元創意藝術（混合媒體、拼貼與綜合創作）、塑膠彩、水彩。

教學理念：相信「透過動手、邊做邊觀察」，理解會慢慢成形；以實作帶動思考，配合示範，讓不同年齡學員累積經驗、發展個人視覺語言。授課語言：粵語、英語、普通話。

English

Kenneth Ho Kwan Wong holds a BA in Visual Communication Design (Hong Kong Polytechnic University), an MSc in Sound Design (University of Edinburgh), and a PGCE in Secondary Design and Technology (University of Birmingham). He is a Hong Kong registered teacher and holds UK Qualified Teacher Status (QTS).

His creative path began with visual art in secondary school: he achieved Grade A in HKCEE Visual Art and HKAL Visual Art, and worked from an art-making starting point before extending into visual communication / multimedia design and broader cross-disciplinary practice. He taught Design & Technology in UK secondary schools (including as a Year 7 form tutor), with strong experience in classroom management, technical drawings, assessment, and supporting diverse learners. He returned to Hong Kong in 2026 to teach visual arts in local secondary schools, including HKDSE SBA support for NCS students and units on surrealism, cubism, and minimalism.

Courses offered: Multi-media creative arts (mixed media and collage-led projects), acrylic painting, and watercolour.

Teaching approach: practice-led learning: students learn by doing, noticing how materials and colour behave, and reflecting as they work; with clear demonstrations to build confidence and a personal visual voice. Languages: Cantonese, English, Mandarin.
